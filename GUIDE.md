# Wild Creatures 1.12.0 (Forge 1.20.1)

A creature mod that can keep growing. It has five dragons so far: the **Red Dragon**, the **Frost Dragon**, the **Lightning Dragon**, the **Sea Dragon** and the very rare, tameable **Black Dragon** that you can ride (see v1.9.0 to v1.12.0 at the bottom).
Install: put `wildcreatures-1.12.0.jar` in your `mods` folder (Forge 47 for Minecraft 1.20.1), plus TerraBlender (see the Ashen Wastes section).

## Red Dragon
- Big and hostile: about 13 blocks from nose to tail and about 5 blocks tall, with a slim, athletic body covered in overlapping scales.
- Hunts players and villagers, and spots you even if you're high up on a cliff.
- **Bite:** 16 damage (8 hearts) when you're within about 5 blocks of it. Jaws open wide, then snap.
- **Fire breath:** a 2.5 second stream of fire up to 18 blocks. 7 damage per hit, about twice a second, and sets you on fire for 8 seconds. Everything within 3 blocks of where it aims catches fire: the ground, grass, trees, other mobs. It sweeps toward you a little late, so you can run out of it. Fire Protection and Fire Resistance help.
- **Flying:** walks normally, but takes off when you're far away, high up or out of reach. It flaps after you, bites and breathes fire from the air, then lands again. Sometimes it just takes off and circles for a while.
- 200 health, 10 armor, can't be hurt by fire or lava, takes no fall damage.
- Drops 3 to 6 **Red Dragon Scales** (more with Looting) and lots of XP.
- Spawns rarely, alone, outdoors in mountain and badlands biomes. Never on Peaceful.
- Spawn egg in the **Wild Creatures** creative tab (and the vanilla Spawn Eggs tab).

All of these numbers are starting guesses. Change any of them in `config/wildcreatures-common.toml` (created after the first launch); no rebuild needed. You can also turn off block burning (`breathSetsBlocksOnFire = false`) or flying (`canFly = false`), and make spawns rarer with `spawnRarity`. The breath only burns blocks when the `mobGriefing` game rule is on.

## Dragon sounds
The dragon has its own sounds: two roars and two growls (it picks one at random), hurt cries, a long death roar, a jaw snap when it bites, a roaring whoosh of fire, wing flaps and heavy footsteps. They're made by `tools/make_sounds.py`.

## Dragon Scale armor
Craft it from Red Dragon Scales, using the same shapes as iron armor:
- **Helmet** (5 scales), **Chestplate** (8), **Leggings** (7), **Boots** (4). A full set takes 24 scales, about 4 to 6 dragons.

It's a bit stronger than netherite: 24 armor points for the full set (netherite is 20), 3.5 toughness, more durability, and it doesn't burn in lava. Repair it with scales on an anvil.
It looks like netherite armor recoloured dragon red, with dark crimson plates and warm gold-orange edges, and the helmet has two small curved horns on top.

## For later
- The armor textures and icons are made by `tools/make_armor.py` (it recolours vanilla netherite armor); the helmet horns are in `client/DragonArmorLayers.java`.
- The dragon model and every dragon's skin are generated by `tools/make_dragon.py` (`python3 tools/make_dragon.py`, then rebuild). Each dragon's colours are one entry in its `VARIANTS` list.
- All dragons share one body and one set of moves (`entity/AbstractDragon.java`). A new kind of dragon is a small class in `entity/` that says what its breath does, plus a line in `ModEntities`, a renderer line in `client/ClientSetup`, a colour entry in `tools/make_dragon.py` and a section in `CreatureConfig`.
- A dragon whose attack isn't a stream (like the Lightning Dragon's call) overrides `breathTick` in its class, and `breathPitch` sets where it points its head while attacking. A dragon can spawn more often in thunderstorms: that's the second number in `AbstractDragon.spawnRules(...)` in `ModEntities`.
- A dragon that swims instead of flying (like the Sea Dragon) uses `.swimming(speed)` in its `CreatureConfig` section and fills in the swimming hooks in `AbstractDragon`: `createGroundMoveControl`, `floatsInWater`, `registerWanderGoals`, `swimToward`, `holdPosition` and `swimPose`. Its finned body is the "sea" model in `tools/make_dragon.py` (it becomes `DragonLayer.createSea`); it has the same bones as the winged body, so `DragonModel` animates both.

## Ashen Wastes biome (v1.5.0)

**Needs TerraBlender.** Put TerraBlender for Forge 1.20.1 (version 3.0.1.7 or newer) in your mods folder next to Wild Creatures. It's the standard library biome mods use to add biomes to the normal world. Download: https://modrinth.com/mod/terrablender (pick the Forge 1.20.1 file).

The biome only appears in **newly generated land**. Make a new world, or travel far into chunks you haven't visited.

- Dark ashen soil ground with patches of basalt and blackstone, a hazy brown sky, falling ash and Nether-style ambience.
- **Burnt oak trees:** tall dead trees (9 to 15 blocks) with bare branches and no leaves. They drop burnt oak logs, which make planks, stairs and slabs. Burnt oak doesn't catch fire.
- **Lava pools:** small pools sunk into the ground, ringed with magma blocks.
- **Volcano:** one big volcano per patch of the biome, about 100 blocks across and 50 to 60 blocks tall, with a lava crater and glowing magma streams down the sides.
- **Mobs:** magma cubes, plus the Red Dragon now and then. The dragon only spawns naturally here now (it used to spawn in mountains and badlands).

Finding it: `/locate biome wildcreatures:ashen_wastes` and `/locate structure wildcreatures:volcano`.

How common the biome is: `ashenWastesWeight` in `config/wildcreatures-common.toml` (default 10; higher = more common, 0 = off).

## Burnt oak set, burnt sticks, nests and dragon sizes (v1.6.0)

- **Burnt oak crafting:** logs make planks; planks make stairs, slabs, fences, fence gates, doors, trapdoors, buttons, pressure plates, signs and hanging signs. Burnt planks also work anywhere normal planks do (crafting table, chest, barrel and so on).
- **Burnt sticks:** two burnt oak planks make 4 burnt sticks (not normal sticks). They're used for burnt oak fences, gates and signs, and they also make torches and ladders. Tools still need normal sticks.
- **Burnt bushes:** small charred bushes all over the Ashen Wastes. Breaking one gives 0 to 2 burnt sticks and about a 1 in 6 chance of a burnt oak sapling (better with Fortune). Shears pick up the bush itself.
- **Burnt oak saplings:** plant them on ashen soil, dirt or grass. They grow into the big dead burnt oak trees (bone meal works).
- **Dragon nests:** a ring of burnt wood about 15 blocks across with bones and a chest in the middle. They appear now and then in every biome the Red Dragon lives in (right now the Ashen Wastes; add more biomes in `data/wildcreatures/tags/worldgen/biome/red_dragon_habitat.json`). The chest can hold burnt oak logs, dragon scales, burnt sticks and saplings, bones, iron and gold, and iron tools and armor (sometimes enchanted). There's also a small chance of diamonds or a golden apple. Find one with `/locate structure wildcreatures:dragon_nest`.
- **Dragon sizes:** each dragon rolls a size when it spawns. Small (70% size, 120 health, weaker hits) 30% of the time. Normal (200 health) most of the time. Giant (150% size, 400 health, harder hits, longer and wider fire breath, drops extra scales) 3% of the time. Change these in the config (`smallChancePercent`, `smallHealth`, `giantChancePercent`, `giantHealth`). To summon a specific size: `/summon wildcreatures:red_dragon ~ ~ ~ {DragonSize:"giant"}` (or "small", "normal").

## Dragon tools, Dragon Shield and Dragon Essence (v1.7.0)

All crafted from **Red Dragon Scales** and **burnt sticks**, using the same shapes as vanilla tools. None of them burn in lava. Repair them with scales on an anvil.

| Item | Recipe | Damage | Speed | Durability | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dragon Sword | 2 scales on 1 burnt stick | 9 | 1.4 | 1800 | Big glowing lava-core blade, held bigger than a normal sword. Sets whatever it hits on fire for 4 seconds. |
| Dragon Pickaxe | 3 scales, 2 burnt sticks | 6 | 1.2 | 1800 | Mines a bit faster than diamond. |
| Dragon Axe | 3 scales, 2 burnt sticks | 10 | 1.0 | 1800 | |
| Dragon Shovel | 1 scale, 2 burnt sticks | 6.5 | 1.0 | 1800 | |
| Dragon Shield | 6 scales + 1 Dragon Essence (shield shape, essence where the iron goes) | | | 1200 | Any mob that hits you while you're blocking catches fire for 5 seconds. |

For comparison, a diamond sword does 7 and lasts 1561 uses; a netherite sword does 8 and lasts 2031. A normal shield lasts 336.

The tools look like the vanilla diamond tools recoloured dragon red, with small spikes, ember highlights and charcoal-grey burnt-stick handles. The shield has a scaled red face with a bronze rim, a glowing dragon eye in the middle, two bone horns on top and a burnt-plank back. The eye and the cracks glow in the dark.

**Dragon Essence** (needed for the shield):
- Red Dragons drop it very rarely when a player kills them: 2% for a normal dragon, 1% for a small one, 10% for a giant. Looting raises it by half per level (Looting III makes it 5% / 2.5% / 25%).
- It's in about 1 in 60 dragon nest chests.
- It glows and shimmers like an enchanted item.

Change the numbers in `config/wildcreatures-common.toml`: `dropChancePercent`, `smallDropChancePercent` and `giantDropChancePercent` under `[dragon_essence]`, and `swordBurnSeconds` and `shieldBurnSeconds` under `[dragon_gear]` (0 turns the fire off). The nest chance is in `data/wildcreatures/loot_tables/chests/dragon_nest.json` (the last pool: weight 1 essence against weight 59 empty).

The textures are made by `tools/make_dragon_gear.py`; the shield's 3D shape is in `client/DragonShieldRenderer.java`.

## Fire-proof dragon armor and cooked drops (v1.8.0)

- **Dragon Scale armor:** wearing all four pieces makes you immune to fire. You never catch fire, and lava, fire, magma blocks and fireballs do no damage, so you can swim in lava. This also blocks the Red Dragon's fire breath (its bite still hurts). With fewer than four pieces on, you burn like normal. Lava still slows you down and is hard to see through.
- **Fix:** the dragon's fire breath now counts as fire damage. Before, Fire Resistance potions and Fire Protection didn't help against it, even though this guide said they did. Now they do.
- **Dragon Sword:** animals you kill with it drop their meat already cooked (steak, cooked porkchop, chicken, mutton, rabbit and fish). Animals that burn to death after a hit drop cooked meat too.

Config (`config/wildcreatures-common.toml`): `fireImmunityPieces` (4 = full set; set it lower to need fewer pieces, 0 = off) and `blocksDragonBreath` (false = the dragon's breath still hurts) under `[dragon_armor]`, and `swordCooksDrops` under `[dragon_gear]`.

## New dragon look and the Frost Dragon (v1.9.0)

**New look for every dragon.** Dragons now have slimmer, more athletic bodies: a deep chest and narrow waist, a longer neck and tail, lean legs (the back legs stand up on their toes like a big cat's), and long wings that fold neatly along the sides when they land. The skins are repainted with smooth, blended colours instead of flat blocks of colour: darkest along the back, fading down the sides to a pale belly, with soft overlapping scales, belly plates and veined wings. The Red Dragon is deep crimson fading to a warm orange belly, with gold eyes.

**Frost Dragon**
- White and pale ice blue, with frosted horns and glowing blue eyes. Same size and moves as the Red Dragon: it walks, flies and bites.
- Lives in snowy mountains: snowy slopes, frozen peaks, jagged peaks and ice spikes. Rare, alone, outdoors only, never on Peaceful.
- **Frost breath:** a 2.5 second stream of ice up to 18 blocks. 5 damage per hit, about twice a second, and you're slowed right down (Slowness II) until 5 seconds after the last hit. It also freezes you like powder snow does: frosty screen edges, blue hearts and a little freezing damage. Wearing any piece of leather armor stops the freezing part, but not the slowness.
- Within 3 blocks of where it aims, water turns to ice, lava turns to obsidian, fires go out and snow piles up on the ground. Burning mobs get put out. Like the Red Dragon's fire, this needs the `mobGriefing` game rule.
- Blazes, striders and magma cubes take 5 times the damage from it.
- 180 health, 8 armor, 14 damage bite. It never freezes and can walk on powder snow, but unlike the Red Dragon it burns in fire and lava.
- Same sizes as the Red Dragon: small (110 health) and giant (360 health).
- Drops 3 to 6 **Frost Dragon Scales** (more with Looting), lots of XP, and the same rare Dragon Essence chance as the Red Dragon. The scales aren't in any recipe yet; they're there for frost gear later.
- Its own sounds: a slightly higher roar and an icy, crackling breath.
- Spawn egg in the Wild Creatures creative tab. `/summon wildcreatures:frost_dragon ~ ~ ~ {DragonSize:"giant"}` works like it does for the Red Dragon.
- Dragon Scale armor doesn't protect you from frost; its immunity only covers fire.

Config: the new `[frost_dragon]` section in `config/wildcreatures-common.toml` has the same settings as `[red_dragon]` (health, sizes, bite, breath, flying, spawn rarity), plus `frostRadius`, `slowSeconds` and `breathFreezesBlocks`. The biomes it spawns in are listed in `data/wildcreatures/tags/worldgen/biome/frost_dragon_habitat.json`.

## The Lightning Dragon (v1.10.0)

- Dark navy blue fading to bright blue down its sides, with a gold belly, gold spikes and gold horns. Gold zigzag lightning bolts run along its neck, back and tail, and they glow in the dark. Same body and size as the other dragons: it walks, flies and bites.
- Lives in windswept mountains: windswept hills, windswept gravelly hills, windswept forest, windswept savanna and stony peaks. Rare, alone, outdoors only, never on Peaceful. During a thunderstorm it spawns 4 times as often.
- **Lightning call** instead of a breath: it rears its head up and roars at the sky, sparks crackle around its head, and a ring of sparks appears on the ground where you're standing. Half a second later, lightning strikes the middle of the ring. Each call strikes 3 times, about 0.6 seconds apart, and each new ring follows you (a little late). It can call from about 20 blocks away, then waits 7 to 10 seconds before calling again.
- A strike does 8 damage (4 hearts) to everything within 3 blocks of it and sets it on fire for 4 seconds. Sprint out of the sparks and it misses. If you only walk, it can still catch you.
- Strikes set the ground on fire like normal lightning (on Normal and Hard, when the `mobGriefing` and `doFireTick` game rules are on).
- **Lightning rods protect you.** A lightning rod within 16 blocks of where it aims, with open sky above it, pulls the strike to the rod. Those strikes never start fires, and the rod gives off its usual redstone pulse.
- Its strikes count as real lightning for mobs: villagers turn into witches, pigs into zombified piglins, and creepers become charged creepers.
- Lightning can't hurt it. A storm's lightning or a Channeling trident heals it a little instead.
- 190 health, 9 armor, 15 damage bite, and it flies a bit faster than the other dragons. Same sizes as the others: small (115 health) and giant (380 health, a wider strike circle and harder strikes).
- Drops 3 to 6 **Lightning Dragon Scales** (more with Looting), lots of XP, and the same rare Dragon Essence chance as the other dragons. The scales aren't in any recipe yet.
- Its own sounds: a deeper roar, a rising electric buzz when it calls the storm, and a crackle when the sparks appear.
- Spawn egg in the Wild Creatures creative tab. `/summon wildcreatures:lightning_dragon ~ ~ ~ {DragonSize:"giant"}` works like it does for the others.
- Dragon Scale armor doesn't stop the strike's damage, but with the full set on you won't catch fire from it.

Config: the new `[lightning_dragon]` section in `config/wildcreatures-common.toml` has the same settings as the other dragons, plus `strikeRadius`, `burnSeconds` and `strikesStartFires`. In that section, `breathDamage` is the damage of one strike, `breathDurationTicks` is how long a call lasts (40 ticks gives 3 strikes; every 12 more ticks adds one) and `breathRange` sets how far away it can call from. The biomes it spawns in are listed in `data/wildcreatures/tags/worldgen/biome/lightning_dragon_habitat.json`.

## The Sea Dragon (v1.11.0)

- Dark navy teal along its back, fading to turquoise down its sides and a pale sea-green belly, with two rows of glowing spots along each side like a deep-sea fish. Instead of spikes it has a ridged fin along its back and tail, a forked tail fin, and fan-shaped frills behind its jaw. Glowing aqua eyes. Same skeleton and size as the other dragons.
- Lives in deep oceans: deep ocean, deep cold ocean, deep lukewarm ocean and deep frozen ocean. While you're out over one, expect one to turn up roughly every couple of minutes, on the sea floor 32 to 64 blocks away and at least 8 blocks down, day or night. Only one at a time: no new one turns up while another is within about 100 blocks of you. Never on Peaceful, and not when the `doMobSpawning` game rule is off. It leaves again once you're about 100 blocks away (unless it's chasing you).
- **Swims fast**, about as fast as a boat at full speed, rowing with its wings like a sea turtle while its tail sweeps from side to side. It tips its nose up to rise and down to dive. It breathes underwater and currents don't push it around.
- It spots you from far below and **rises up to attack**, surfacing right next to your boat.
- **Water blast** instead of a breath: a 2.5 second jet of water up to 20 blocks, in or out of the water. 5 damage per hit, about twice a second, and every hit shoves you back hard and **knocks you out of your boat** (or off your horse).
- The first hit of each blast gives you **Undertow** for 3 seconds: while you're in water it drags you down. Swim up against it and you only sink about 4 blocks; stop swimming and it pulls you a lot deeper. It waits 7 to 10 seconds between blasts, so you always get time to swim back up. A shield blocks the whole blast: no damage, no shove, no Undertow.
- Where the blast lands (within 2.5 blocks), fires and campfires go out, still lava cools into obsidian and flowing lava into cobblestone. Burning mobs get put out. Like the other dragons' breath, the block changes need the `mobGriefing` game rule.
- **Bite:** 14 damage (7 hearts).
- It crawls out onto land after you, but it's slow there (about half speed). When there's nothing left to chase it heads back to the nearest water, even if that means dropping off a cliff into the sea.
- It counts as a sea creature, so tridents with Impaling hurt it more.
- 170 health, 8 armor. Same sizes as the others: small (100 health) and giant (340 health).
- Drops 3 to 6 **Sea Dragon Scales** (more with Looting), lots of XP, and the same rare Dragon Essence chance as the other dragons. The scales aren't in any recipe yet.
- Its own sounds: a deeper, whale-like voice, and a rushing, bubbling roar for the blast.
- Spawn egg in the Wild Creatures creative tab. `/summon wildcreatures:sea_dragon ~ ~ ~ {DragonSize:"giant"}` works like it does for the others.
- Dragon Scale armor doesn't protect you from the blast; its immunity only covers fire.

**Fix for every dragon:** flying dragons now tip their nose up when they climb and down when they dive. It was the wrong way round before.

Config: the new `[sea_dragon]` section in `config/wildcreatures-common.toml` has the same settings as the other dragons, except `swimSpeed` (its top swimming speed when it chases you) instead of `canFly` and `flySpeed`. It also has `blastRadius`, `undertowSeconds` and `blastPutsOutFires`. Its `spawnRarity` works a little differently: every 30 seconds each player gets a 1 in `spawnRarity` chance of a Sea Dragon turning up nearby (4 means about one every two minutes; 0 turns them off). The biomes it spawns in are listed in `data/wildcreatures/tags/worldgen/biome/sea_dragon_habitat.json`.

Why it has its own spawner: normal mob spawning gives every kind of monster a share of one limit, and in an ocean the zombies, skeletons and creepers in the caves underneath always use it up first, so a Sea Dragon never got a turn. `world/SeaDragonSpawner.java` spawns it on its own timer instead. A future sea creature can reuse the same idea.

## The Black Dragon (v1.12.0)

- Jet black with a faint violet sheen, and glowing violet cracks running over its body like embers under black rock. They glow in the dark, along with its violet eyes. Violet tips on its spikes and horns, and dark violet wings.
- **Extremely rare.** It lives alone on the highest mountain peaks (jagged peaks, frozen peaks and stony peaks). Once a minute, each player up in the peaks has a 1 in 40 chance of one landing on a peak 24 to 48 blocks away, under open sky, so expect to explore the peaks for a good while before you see one. Only one at a time: no new one turns up while another (wild or tamed) is within 128 blocks of you. It's friendly, so it can turn up on Peaceful too, but not when the `doMobSpawning` game rule is off. A wild one leaves again once you're about 100 blocks away.
- **Friendly.** It leaves players, villagers and animals alone, and only fights back if you hurt it. Iron golems and snow golems leave it alone too.
- **Taming:** right-click it with raw or cooked meat or fish. Each one has a 1 in 3 chance of winning it over: smoke means not yet, hearts mean it trusts you now.
- **A tamed Black Dragon:**
  - follows you around, and flies after you when you fly, ride off or get far ahead. If you get more than 64 blocks away it catches up by turning up next to you;
  - fights whatever attacks you and whatever you attack, but never you, your pets or your other dragons;
  - sneak and right-click it to make it stay where it is (and again to make it follow). Before it has a saddle, a plain right-click does this too;
  - feed it meat or fish to heal it;
  - never despawns, and you get a message if it dies.
- **Saddle:** right-click it with a normal saddle. A leather seat with a gold-trimmed purple saddle cloth and stirrups appears on its back. The saddle drops if it dies.
- **Riding:** right-click it with an empty hand (or anything that isn't food or a saddle) to climb on. Only you, its owner, can ride it.
  - W, A, S, D walk and turn on the ground. It turns to face where you look.
  - **Space** takes off, and climbs while you hold it.
  - In the air it flies where you look: look up to climb and down to dive (it stays level unless you look well up or down). Let go of everything and it glides slowly down.
  - **Shift** dives. It doesn't throw you off up there. Once it has landed, Shift gets you off as normal.
  - It lands by itself when it touches the ground.
  - If you get off high up, you get Slow Falling so you float down safely.
  - Climbing on switches you to the view from behind, with the camera pulled back so you see the whole dragon. F5 still changes the view, and getting off puts your old view back.
- **Shadow flame:** left-click while riding to breathe a stream of black fire at whatever you're looking at, up to 22 blocks. 8 damage about twice a second, plus **Wither II** for 4 seconds. Wither skeletons (and the Wither) shrug off the wither but still take the damage. It lasts about 2.5 seconds, then it needs about 2.5 seconds before it can breathe again. Grass and flowers it lands on wither away: plants crumble (some leave a dead bush) and grass, podzol, mycelium and moss turn to bare dirt (needs `mobGriefing`). The Black Dragon can't be withered itself. A wild one uses its breath when it fights back.
- **Bite:** 18 damage (9 hearts).
- 260 health and 14 armor, the toughest dragon so far. Same sizes as the others: small (160 health) and giant (480 health).
- Drops 3 to 6 **Black Dragon Scales** (more with Looting), lots of XP, and the same rare Dragon Essence chance as the other dragons. The scales aren't in any recipe yet.
- A deeper voice than the other dragons, and a hollow, roaring rush of a breath sound.
- Spawn egg in the Wild Creatures creative tab.

Config: the new `[black_dragon]` section has the usual dragon settings plus `tameChancePercent` (the chance each piece of food tames it, 33 by default), `riddenFlySpeed` (its top speed in the air with you on it), `witherSeconds` and `flameWithersPlants`. `spawnRarity` is the 1 in N chance per player per minute (0 turns them off). The biomes are listed in `data/wildcreatures/tags/worldgen/biome/black_dragon_habitat.json`.
